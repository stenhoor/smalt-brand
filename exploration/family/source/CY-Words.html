smaltcy: Cypher for smaltdb

smaltcy is the Cypher query language for smaltdb: patterns of nodes and relationships, matched over the database's tables. It joins smaltql, smaltdf and smaltts in the language layer (cobalt magenta), and it arrives once smaltdb supports graph queries and graph table structures. Words describe what it is; no comparisons with other graph databases.
