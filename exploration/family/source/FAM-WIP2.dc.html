Come build with us: a sign as clear as the barrier

The barrier (picked) reads at a glance; the round 1 hard hats didn't. Two better hard hats, and two other signs of building. Each is shown beside the barrier, at 48, 24 and 16 px on dark, and as the two separate badges.

K1 · Hard hat, side view

A round crown with the ridge down the middle and the brim's peak at the front: the details that make it a hard hat, not a bowl. Breaks the angle rule with one curve.
