Five marks for smaltkit

Small. Alternative. Reusable. smaltkit is the shared browser code the smalt apps are built from: editor, grid, charts, graph view, controls. Drawn by the family rules: solid bars, square-cut ends, 0/45/90° angles, one chip, here in the kit layer's jade (as smaltml was placed).
