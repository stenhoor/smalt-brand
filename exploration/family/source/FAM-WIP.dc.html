Work in progress. Come build with us.

Two icons for the preview label: a sign of work in progress (cone, tape or barrier) and an invitation to join in (a hard hat). Drawn in smalt with kiln orange, the colour construction gear already uses, which is smaltworks' own accent. Each is shown large, then at 48, 24 and 16 px on dark. Below, the badge with each pairing.

WORK IN PROGRESS

W1 · Traffic cone

A cone drawn at the family's 45°, so it is squat. Kiln tip and stripe, smalt bands.

W2 · Warning tape

A band of 45° hazard stripes, smalt and kiln. Fits the drawing rules exactly; reads at any size.

W3 · Barrier

The striped board on two legs that closes a road for work. The most "under construction".

COME BUILD WITH US

H1 · Hard hat

A round dome in kiln on a smalt brim: the classic outline. The only curve in the family's icons.

H2 · Hard hat, faceted

The dome drawn with 45° corners, by the family rules. Kiln dome, smalt brim.

H3 · Hard hat with ridge

A smalt dome split by a kiln ridge: the chip idea, but busier at 16 px.
