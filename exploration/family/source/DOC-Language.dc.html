SELECT

Reads rows: from tables, views and subqueries, filtered, grouped and ordered.

Syntax

select-core (1 of 2)
select-core (2 of 2)
result-column ▾ (expanded in place)

Boxes with rounded ends are keywords; square boxes are rules, and open in place. The diagrams show the grammar; the rules below say what it can't, such as where an aggregate may appear.

Rules

FROM runs first, then WHERE on each row, then GROUP BY, then HAVING on each group, then the result columns, then ORDER BY and LIMIT. An aggregate may appear in the result columns and in HAVING, never in WHERE.
