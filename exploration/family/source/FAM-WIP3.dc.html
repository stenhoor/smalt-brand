A hard hat that looks like one

Rounds 1 and 2 got the hat wrong: a gap between shell and brim, the rib drawn as a split, a flat dome. The 0/45/90° rule can't draw a dome, so these signs drop it: they are signage, not product marks. Each is shown beside the barrier, at 48, 24 and 16 px on dark, and as the badge.

A · Front view, one colour

A full dome on a brim that reaches past it, and the raised rib over the crown, all in kiln orange, the way hard hats are one colour. The rib and the brim's edge are cut as thin lines.
