The standard, in eight rules

What the other boards show, written down. Once you pick, it goes into BRAND.md as a section beside "Sites and READMEs", and the components go into the site kit.

StructureEvery site: Quick start, Getting started, User guide, Reference, in that order, with those names (NumPy). Home opens on the four cards.
Quick startOne page, five minutes, three steps at most, ending in a live playground: something real runs.
Reference entriesOne shape for every function, method and statement: signature with types, one-line summary, parameters, returns, errors with codes, notes, examples with printed results, working demo, see also (Polars).
Syntax diagramsEvery statement and grammar rule of a language (smaltql, smaltdf's chains) has a railroad diagram; rules link and expand in place; prose says what the diagram can't (SQLite).
Examples are testsEvery example runs in CI and its printed result is checked, as smaltql's docs do today.
Working demosEvery language and API page ends with a complete, small program (data, code, result) that runs as written.
PlaygroundsOn every guide and reference page, from the site kit; built from smaltkit; the site's own engine; nothing loaded until Run.
VoiceBRAND.md as always: what it is, short sentences, the work-in-progress badge, no numbers without their benchmark.

For you to decide

Syntax diagramsOur own generator (recommended: small, ours, styled from the tokens; the mock-ups use it), or Mermaid's railroad diagrams (beta since v11.16, outside code, needs your yes).
Rule 12It says nothing on an app site but Demo opens the app. Playgrounds aren't the app; add a line saying so.
