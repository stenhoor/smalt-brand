smaltlp: the best answer within your constraints

smaltlp is linear and mixed-integer programming for the smalt family, in TypeScript, in the browser: a model of costs, limits and choices, solved to its best answer. Its scope is smaltworks ADR 0039. A kit with a brand of its own, like smaltml, in the Kits layer's jade.

What the words have to cover

Where smaltlp sits and what its words promise, from ADR 0039.

Where it sits
the Kits layer, beside smaltml
A library, not part of the engine. Notebooks, apps and flows call it; queries reach it through a plugin function later.
What it solves
linear programs, then integer ones
LP first; mixed-integer programs next. Quadratic programs and constraint programming are left out.
Exact answers
a dual simplex in 64-bit floats
Vertex solutions with duals and reduced costs, compared with HiGHS. WebGPU only where it measures faster.
Its ancestors
PuLP · scipy · HiGHS · GLPK · OR-Tools
Models built like PuLP's, results like scipy's linprog and milp; our own code, from the papers.
Private
the data stays on the machine
Models and their data are solved in the browser, where they already are.
