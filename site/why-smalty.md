# Why smalty

## The name

**Smalt** is a pigment: cobalt glass, melted in a kiln and ground to a blue powder. Painters used it for centuries,
and the brand's blue, `#003399`, is smalt's own colour. A *smalt works* was the factory that made it, which is where
**smaltworks** gets its name.

Read it another way and it says what we make: **sm**all + **alt**ernative. Every product name keeps that `alt` in
smalt blue: sm<span class="sw-alt">alt</span>db, sm<span class="sw-alt">alt</span>ui, sm<span class="sw-alt">alt</span>bi,
sm<span class="sw-alt">alt</span>df, sm<span class="sw-alt">alt</span>nb.

Smalt rhymes with salt, and that's where the fun starts.

## Smalty, not salty

*Salty* means annoyed. *Smalty* is the opposite: having fun doing our own thing.

**Stay smalty** is a nod to salty and a counterpoint to it. It's the slightly irreverent streak in everything we
make. Much of the tooling for working with data today comes as separate pieces that were never designed together.
We're rebuilding those pieces as one family, around one engine, because we think it's more fun, and more useful, that
way.

Our community members are **smalties**, and they're the **smalt of the earth**.

## Small data deserves good tools

Big data has had decades of attention. Small data, the data that fits on the machine in front of you, has mostly been
left with whatever was lying around. We believe that's where most of the world's everyday data work actually happens:
the CSV export, the overgrown spreadsheet, the folder of Parquet files, the database someone needs to look inside.

So we build for that. Tools that start in a browser tab, run on the hardware you already have, and keep the barrier
to entry as small as the name.

## Inspired by, not against

A lot of what we make stands on ideas from tools we've loved using, and we say so:

- **smaltui** is a query workbench in the spirit of Toad.
- **smaltbi** takes its cue from Power BI and Tableau.
- **smaltnb** is inspired by marimo, Jupyter and JupyterLab.
- **smaltql** follows SQLite's SQL dialect.
- **smaltdf** takes its functions' inspiration from Polars, and owes a lot to pandas. It is its own language, with its own reference.

We're not here to replace any of them. What we add is one family built to work together, all in the browser, for
small data.

## How we talk

- **Say what things are,** not what they aren't.
- **Don't pick fights.** We credit the tools that shaped us and never knock them.
- **No numbers without sources.** A benchmark figure appears only with its method beside it.
- **Keep it light.** A little sass, never at anyone's expense.

The full rules are in the [brand book](https://github.com/stenhoor/smalt-brand/blob/main/BRAND.md).
