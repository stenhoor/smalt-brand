# <Mark product="bi" :size="40" /> smaltbi

<StageBadge />

**Small. Alternative. Insight.** Business intelligence in the browser, powered by smaltdb.

smaltbi takes its cue from Power BI and Tableau. It's built for analysts who want answers without writing queries,
and keeps SQL underneath for those who do.

- **Load** CSV, JSON or Parquet files, or open a smaltdb database.
- **Model** them with relationships and SQL measures, and add a date table for year to date, last year and rolling
  months without writing SQL.
- **Design** reports by dragging fields onto a canvas, with cross-filtering, drill-down and conditional formatting.
- **Publish** a report as a static site that runs entirely in its reader's browser, as a responsive page, a fixed
  slide or A4 page, or one column on a phone.

**Status:** 0.2.0, in development: the designer, viewer, model and static publishing work end to end, with time
intelligence, conditional formatting, fixed page sizes and phone layouts.
