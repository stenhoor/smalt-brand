# Contact

smaltworks makes the smalt family in the open, on GitHub. That is where to reach us.

## Questions and ideas

Start a conversation in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/stenhoor/smalt-brand/discussions): questions, ideas,
and what you are building with smalt.

## Bugs and requests

Each product has its own issue tracker. [Get involved](/community/get-involved) lists them all.

## Security {#security}

Report a vulnerability privately through GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/stenhoor/smalt-brand/security/advisories/new), not in a public
issue or discussion. We reply there. The site's [`security.txt`](/.well-known/security.txt) says the same.
