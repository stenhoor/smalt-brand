# Privacy

smaltworks.dev is a static site. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no advertising, and it sets no cookies.

## What reaches others when you visit

- **GitHub** serves the site through GitHub Pages and keeps server logs of requests, such as your IP address, as
  GitHub's [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  describes.
- **Google Fonts** serves the site's type (Geist, Geist Mono and Martian Mono). Your browser asks Google for them, so
  Google receives your IP address and browser details, as its
  [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) describes.

## What stays in your browser

- Your light or dark theme choice, kept in your browser's local storage (`vitepress-theme-appearance`). It never
  leaves your device.
- The site search runs in your browser, on an index downloaded with the site. What you type is not sent anywhere.

## The smalt products

The smalt products run in your browser too: your data stays on your device unless you choose to send it somewhere. A
product's own site says what it loads.
