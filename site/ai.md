# How smaltworks works with AI

Most developers don't love code for its own sake. They love to create, and code has been how they do it.

We are developers, and we love to create. AI lets us build at scale and test ideas faster and better than we ever
could by hand, so smaltworks works AI-first.

We build tools and libraries that help other people create, whether they write every line by hand or work AI-first.
We love developers and the work they do, and everything we make is meant to help them create.

**Built AI-first, by developers, for everyone who creates.**

## What that looks like in practice

- **Kits are built for agents as well as people.** Each [kit](/products/kits) is small and focused, with few options,
  so an AI agent, even a smaller one, has fewer choices to get wrong and produces consistent results.
- **Everything is plain and inspectable.** SQL you can read, plans you can explain, files you can open. Whether a
  person or an agent wrote it, you can check it.
- **The tools work the same either way.** Nothing in the family requires AI to use it.
